# Department of Justice

> The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) enforces federal law and defends the interests of the
> United States. It publishes a small portfolio of public, read-only APIs and machine-readable
> data catalogs: the DOJ News API for press releases and blog entries, the National FOIA Portal
> JSON:API on api.foia.gov, the Bureau of Justice Statistics NCVS and NIBRS National Estimates
> datasets on api.ojp.gov, the FARA e-File registrant feeds, and a DCAT-US 1.1 data catalog at
> justice.gov/data.json.

Generated 2026-09-06 by API Evangelist from apis.yml and the artifacts in this repository.
Source of record: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/department-of-justice/refs/heads/main/apis.yml

## APIs

- [DOJ News API](https://www.justice.gov/developer/api-documentation/api_v1): Press releases and
  blog entries from the Office of Public Affairs as JSON. Base https://www.justice.gov/api/v1.
  Documented resources: GET /api/v1/press_releases.json, GET /api/v1/press_releases/[uuid].json,
  GET /api/v1/blog_entries.json, GET /api/v1/blog_entries/[uuid].json. No API key required.
- [National FOIA Portal JSON:API](https://www.foia.gov/swagger.html): 24 read operations over
  agency components, agency taxonomy, annual and quarterly FOIA reports, annual report XML, and
  Chief FOIA Officers Council meetings, committees, councils and pages. Base
  https://api.foia.gov/api. Requires an api.data.gov key in an X-API-Key header. Media type
  application/vnd.api+json (JSON:API).
- [BJS National Crime Victimization Survey (NCVS) API](https://bjs.ojp.gov/national-crime-victimization-survey-ncvs-api):
  NCVS Select personal and household population and victimization datasets in JSON, CSV and XML.
  Base https://api.ojp.gov/bjsdataset/v1. Socrata-style resource ids with a $limit parameter.
- [BJS NIBRS National Estimates API](https://bjs.ojp.gov/national-incident-based-reporting-system-nibrs-national-estimates-api):
  National incident, offense and victim-level NIBRS estimates. Base
  https://api.ojp.gov/bjsdataset/v1.
- [FARA e-File Registrant API](https://www.justice.gov/developer): Foreign Agents Registration Act
  registrant data. Base https://efile.fara.gov/api/v1.
- [DOJ Open Data Catalog](https://www.justice.gov/data): 3,267 DOJ datasets described in
  DCAT-US 1.1 at https://www.justice.gov/data.json, mirrored to Data.gov under the doj-gov
  organization.

## Standards

- JSON:API 1.0 — the FOIA API declares application/vnd.api+json and is built on the Drupal
  JSON:API module.
- NIEM — the FOIA Annual Report XML schema conforms to the National Information Exchange Model.
- DCAT-US 1.1 / Project Open Data — justice.gov/data.json declares
  conformsTo https://project-open-data.cio.gov/v1.1/schema.
